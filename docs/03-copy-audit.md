# Copy audit: what sounded AI-generated, and what replaced it

Checked 14 November 2026 against Hallmark's copy gates (19 generated voice, 54 eyebrows, 58 screen budget, 63 meta strings, 69 filler) and three plain tests: can a reader picture it, could it be proven false, and could only this clinic say it.

## Tells found across the site

| Pattern | Where it appeared | Why it reads as generated |
|---|---|---|
| Tracked-caps eyebrow over every heading ("OUR TREATMENTS", "HOW IT WORKS", "WHY THE DERMA HOUSE") | Every section, every page | The single most common AI page pattern |
| One italic gold word in the headline ("Glow every day with *renewed* confidence") | Home hero | Classic generated accent |
| Tricolons ("Personalised care, advanced techniques, visible results.") | Home | Three abstract nouns, no facts |
| "Not just X" constructions ("follow-ups so the results last, not just the first week", "the cause, not just the symptoms") | Home, Skin | Gate 19 phrase |
| Clichés: "under one roof", "Ready to transform…?", "Take the first step toward…", "No guesswork", "Nourish your body. Support your health." | About, closing bands, Nutrition | Fit any clinic anywhere |
| Floating stat cards on the hero photo ("43 treatments across…", "Reply within 24 hours") | Home hero | Decorative device, not content |
| Five-star rows on testimonials | Home | A rating nobody gave (invented metric) |
| Icon-in-a-circle benefit grid (4 checkmarks) | Home, About | Template filler |
| Numbered "01 02 03 04" step cards | Home | Generic process band |
| Arrows glued to links ("Browse all treatments →") | Site-wide | Gate 63 |
| Middle-dot meta strings ("M.Sc. Food, Nutrition & Dietetics · Clinical Nutritionist") | Home, Nutrition | Gate 63 |

## Kept as found, for the clinic to verify

- **The five testimonials.** They are carried over word for word from the old site, because a quote can't be edited. They read as generic and possibly written by the previous agency: similar length, each ends with an exclamation, each names a different service, with no dates or sources. Confirm they are real patients before launch, or replace them with Google reviews quoted with permission.
- **Claims:** "certified dermatologists", "FDA-approved equipment", "thousands of satisfied clients" (now worded "The clinic says it has treated thousands of patients"), "reply within 24 hours".
- **The 43 treatment descriptions** are the clinic's own short definitions and were left as they were.
