The Derma House Skin Clinic strives to provide the highest standard of patient care. Because of the medical nature of our services, the following refund and return policy applies.

## Consultations

- All consultation fees are non-refundable, whether or not treatment is pursued.

## Treatment services

- Payments for dermatological or aesthetic treatments are non-refundable once rendered.
- No refunds are given for a perceived lack of results if procedures were completed according to clinical standards and the consented plan.
- In rare cases of adverse reactions, the clinic may offer alternative treatment or credit toward another service, subject to medical evaluation.

## Prepaid packages and subscriptions

- If no sessions have been used, a refund request may be considered within 30 days of purchase, subject to a 10% administrative fee.
- Once sessions are used, partial refunds are not available.
- Transfers to another person may be allowed on written request and approval.

## Product returns

- Unopened, unused products in original condition may be returned within 10 days of purchase with a valid receipt.
- Opened, used or damaged items are non-returnable and non-refundable.
- Prescription-based or custom-formulated products are strictly non-refundable.

## Missed appointments and late cancellations

- Fees for late cancellations and no-shows are non-refundable.
- Prepaid slots missed without proper notice count as used sessions.

## Payment disputes

- Disputes about charges must be raised in writing within 10 days of the transaction date.
- All refund claims are reviewed within 7 business days.

## Mode of refund

- Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method only.
- Refunds may take 5–7 business days to reflect, depending on your bank or payment provider.
