## Who we are

The Derma House Skin Clinic is owned and operated by Kakkar Healthcare, Kaithal, Haryana, India. Website: [dermahouseclinic.com](https://dermahouseclinic.com). For privacy matters, contact our Grievance Officer at [tdhskinclinic@gmail.com](mailto:tdhskinclinic@gmail.com). We respond within applicable legal timelines.

## What personal data we collect

- **Contact and booking data:** name, phone number, email and message contents you send through the booking form, WhatsApp, email or phone.
- **Technical data:** basic server logs such as IP address, device and browser details, pages viewed and timestamps.
- **Optional health details** you choose to share in free text. We treat these as sensitive and keep them to a minimum.

## How the booking form works

The booking form on this website does not store your details on our web server. When you press send, it opens WhatsApp with your message pre-filled, and your request reaches us only once you send it. WhatsApp's own privacy terms apply to messages sent through it.

## Why we collect it

- To process appointment requests and respond to enquiries.
- To provide and secure our services.
- To comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud or abuse.

## Consent and lawful basis

We process your data with your consent, to provide the services you request, and for legitimate purposes such as security, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable Indian law.

## Third-party services

This website uses Google Maps (to show our location) and Google Fonts (for typography), which may receive your IP address when the page loads. Hosting, email and messaging providers that help us run the site and respond to you are bound by agreements requiring reasonable security.

## Data retention

- Contact and booking records: usually kept up to 24 months from your last interaction, unless the law or ongoing treatment requires longer.
- Security logs: kept for a limited period to detect and prevent abuse.

## Security safeguards

HTTPS encryption in transit, role-based access on a least-privilege basis, activity logging and staff confidentiality obligations. Our vendors must maintain comparable protections.

## Your rights

You can request access to, correction of or deletion of your data, and withdraw consent at any time, by emailing [tdhskinclinic@gmail.com](mailto:tdhskinclinic@gmail.com).

## Children

Our services are intended for adults. If a guardian believes a child has shared data with us, please contact us and we will remove it.

## Updates

We may update this notice and will post the new effective date on this page.
